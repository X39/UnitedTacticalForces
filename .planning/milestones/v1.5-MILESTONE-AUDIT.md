## Verdict

Passed.

- Provider teardown remains separate from runtime stop and runtime cleanup.
- Policy evaluation and dry evaluation remain provider-mutation-free.
- Warning acknowledgement and approval exceptions are durable and scoped.
- Policy visibility endpoints use admin or resource-based authorization.
- Secret-bearing provider/worker credentials are not exposed through Phase 24 visibility DTO field names.
