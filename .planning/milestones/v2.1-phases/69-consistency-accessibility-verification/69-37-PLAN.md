- Design decision (reversible): the regions use explicit `aria-live` (polite and assertive) with `aria-atomic="false"` and `aria-relevant="additions text"` and carry no role. The aria-live variant keeps the fix (the region exists before its content) and fails only the toast tests listed below. UI-SPEC O-09 says "role status"; this plan records the polite region as its implementation.
