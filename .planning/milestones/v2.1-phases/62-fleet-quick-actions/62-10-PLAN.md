<objective>
Build the three small behavior hooks the fleet page composes: the submitted-card tracker (D-12), the focus fallback for cards that move between sections, and the polite state-change announcements. Each is isolated, deterministic and testable without the page.

Purpose: these are the parts of FLT-04's "card updates without a reload" experience that are not rendering: the card must show a working state after a confirmed action without flipping its status optimistically, focus must survive a card changing sections, and a screen-reader user must hear that a server changed. Keeping them out of the page keeps the page a composition.

Output: `useFleetSubmitted`, `useFleetFocusFallback`, `useFleetAnnouncements` and three test files.
</objective>
