Purpose: this is the visible heart of FLT-01 and FLT-04 and the surface FLT-05 is judged on. The card is a thin rendering of decisions already made by the pure model, so its tests can enumerate every state without a page.

Output: `FleetServerCard`, `FleetSection`, `fleet-card.css` and `fleet-card.test.tsx`.
</objective>
