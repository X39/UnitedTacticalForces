<objective>
Define the worker channel frame contract and execute every channel frame through the unchanged protocol service (tracer-first): a heartbeat frame from a registered worker is validated and persisted through `IWorkerProtocolService.HeartbeatAsync`, then claim and the four command reports become frames with HTTP-equivalent results. The socket transport is plan 67-03; the registry and hardening are plan 67-06.

Purpose: D-01 and D-02 replace polling with a push channel while keeping deployment-scoped credentials, idempotent reports, redelivery and restart recovery. A thin route per frame type in front of `IWorkerProtocolService` makes the rewrite a transport swap, not a re-implementation of the guarantees (RESEARCH Pattern 1). Proving the routes first, at the service level, keeps the socket plan small and its failures transport-only.

Output: three HostingContracts types, `AuthenticatedWorker`, the channel JSON options, the route seam with its request and result records, three routes, the shared `WorkerChannelTestHost` and `WorkerChannelRouteTests`.
</objective>
