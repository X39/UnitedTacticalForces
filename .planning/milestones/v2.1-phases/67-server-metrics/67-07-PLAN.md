<objective>
Serve the retained history and keep it at its retention (tracer-first): a viewer reads the tier series of a range with API-computed aggregates and honest gaps, then a durable background job prunes every tier to its retention.

Purpose: MET-04 asks for a short retained history that survives a reload; D-05 fixes 7 days, downsampled, pruned by a durable background job; D-09 forbids filling gaps. The REST endpoint over this read is plan 67-12.

Output: `EServerMetricsRange`, the range helpers on `ServerMetricResolutions`, three history DTOs, `GetHistoryAsync`, `PruneAsync`, the hosted prune worker and its registration, and tests.
</objective>
