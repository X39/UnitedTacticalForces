<objective>
Make a worker metrics sample a durable, honest fact in the control plane (tracer-first): one primary-worker sample is validated, attributed and stored as the server's snapshot, then it is merged into the 15 s, 60 s and 300 s rollup tiers with spike-preserving minimum and maximum and guarded against foreign, future, stale and contradictory samples.

Purpose: D-04 and D-05 require durable, downsampled history that survives reloads and API restarts with spikes preserved; MET-05 requires that an unmeasured reading is a reason, never a number. The availability read model is plan 67-04 (with the migration), the history read and pruning are plan 67-07.

Output: two HostingContracts types, two entities with DbSets, the ingest half of the metrics service with its options, resolutions, source and result records, and tests.
</objective>
