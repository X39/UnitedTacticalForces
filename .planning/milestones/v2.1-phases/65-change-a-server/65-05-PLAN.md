<objective>
Give the mod pack chooser the data D-05 asks for and make a pack change safe. The tracer adds a read-only, server-scoped contents endpoint end to end (DTOs, service on the existing resolver, controller route, tests); then mod pack options gain availability with a reason and keep the saved pack visible, and `ChangeModPackAsync` clears the stale pin, validates resolvability with the same reason and marks the change as pending.

Purpose: the options DTO has only `PrimaryKey` and `Title` today, lists only active packs (a deactivated saved pack vanishes from the chooser) and `ChangeModPackAsync` never clears `PinnedModPackRevisionFk`, which makes a server created with a pinned revision unstartable after a pack change ("A selected mod pack has no resolvable revision.", RESEARCH Pitfall 3).

Output: contents DTOs, content service and route, availability on options, the safe pack change and tests.
</objective>
