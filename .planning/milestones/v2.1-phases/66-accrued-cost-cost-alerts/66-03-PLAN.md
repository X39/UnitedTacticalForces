<objective>
Make a threshold crossing a durable, once-only fact (tracer-first): the cost-alert tables, the per-owner thresholds, the crossing evaluator that records an alert and its per-channel delivery intents in one transaction, then the tool-generated migration (blocking schema gate), then the hosted worker that runs the evaluator on a cadence with group-owner recipients.

Purpose: success criterion 4 requires each crossing to alert once per channel across API restarts (D-14), and D-09 requires the in-app alert even without a deliverable address. Persisting the crossing and the delivery intents together, behind a unique index, is the same persist-intent-first discipline the provider and finance code use.

Output: five entities and two enums, `User.Locale` (consumed by plans 66-06 and 66-07), the evaluator with its result records, the recipient resolver, `CostAlertOptions`, the `AddCostAlerts` migration, `CostAlertWorker`, and tests.
</objective>
