<objective>
Prove the accrued-cost path end to end on the control plane (tracer-first): durable ledger slices, a pure prorating calculator, a scoped service that picks the server's latest session and applies the cost-owner visibility rule, a DTO and a REST endpoint. Then lock the calculator with an edge-case matrix and add the cost-owner view audience that later plans use for value frames and the in-app alert audience.

Purpose: CST-03 needs a number the control plane owns (D-01, D-02). Slices are charged in advance (15-minute reservations renewed before expiry), so summing them overstates cost and adding a prorated in-progress slice double-counts it (RESEARCH Pitfall 1). Prorating every slice by its elapsed fraction, frozen at resolution, is restart-safe and needs no new column.

Output: `CostAccrualCalculator`, `AccruedCostSlice`, `AccruedCostResult`, `AccruedCostUnavailableReasons`, `ICostAccrualService`/`CostAccrualService`, `AccruedCostDto`, `GameServerAccruedCostDto`, the `accrued-cost` endpoint, `GetCostOwnerViewAudienceAsync` with `CostOwnerViewGrant`, and tests.
</objective>
