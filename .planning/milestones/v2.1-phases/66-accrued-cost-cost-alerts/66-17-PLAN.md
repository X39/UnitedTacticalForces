<objective>
Finish the in-app alert surfaces (D-16, D-17, UAT gaps 2 and 3), tracer-first: every accepted dismissal shows a localized success toast that survives the banner disappearing, end to end from the Dismiss button through the API acceptance to the toast region. Output: dismissal toasts, the redesigned `CostAlertBanner` and `CostAlertMenuItem`, CSS, four EN and DE strings, tests, the screen capture extension and the UI-SPEC amendment.
