<objective>
Make servers start and stop on their weekly schedules (tracer-first): a schedule runner records each due occurrence exactly once, queues consented and affordable scheduled starts or records why it skipped them, turns scheduled stops into warned pending stops, and catches up safely after downtime.

Purpose: AUT-03 (the owner schedules start and stop times and the server starts and stops at those times) and the restart half of AUT-06 ("a stop that came due while the API was down still happens exactly once").

Output: the schedule runner and its interface, planner and evaluator extensions, the test factory update, scheduled-run tests.
</objective>

Research A4 (start grace window), A5 (stop due at startup runs without warning, audit says so), rule 6 (relevance: a stop only applies to a deployment already running at the occurrence). Claude's discretion recorded here: each scheduled Start occurrence is processed in its own transaction (run row plus enqueue, or on conflict a fresh transaction recording SkippedBusy), because a conflicting start can leave tracked changes; a due Stop occurrence without a warning row first commits a Pending stop row with `WarnedAt` null in the scan transaction (so the occurrence cannot be lost) and the pending-stop reconcile executes it, retrying while another action is pending; an occurrence superseded by a manual stop is recorded SkippedBusy, one superseded because the server stopped is recorded SkippedOff; a cancelled scheduled stop (rule changed or removed) records no run.
