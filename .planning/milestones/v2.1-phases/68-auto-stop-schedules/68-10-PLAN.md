<objective>
Make servers stop themselves for idle time and maximum runtime (tracer-first): a pure planner decides warn, cancel, supersede or execute; a persistence shell records the warning episode with its alert and audit, withdraws it when the condition ends, and executes it exactly once through the automated lifecycle entry point; a hosted worker runs it.

Purpose: AUT-01, AUT-02 and AUT-04 to AUT-06 meet here. Research Patterns 1, 2 and 4: inject `Instant now`, persist intent first, claim by conditional update, keep the arithmetic pure.

Output: planner types, the evaluator and its result, the worker and its registration, four test classes.
</objective>
