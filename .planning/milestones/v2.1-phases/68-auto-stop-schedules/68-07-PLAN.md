<objective>
Extend the Phase 66 alert stack with an AutoStop kind (tracer-first): a writer that records a warning alert with its deliveries for a pending stop, resolution that withdraws it everywhere, the list, DTO and raised-frame fields the UI needs, and English and German Discord and email text.

Purpose: AUT-04 requires a warning shortly before every automatic stop through the Phase 66 channels; D-AUTO-3 addresses it to the cost-owner alert recipients while the workspace notice reaches every viewer. Research "Don't Hand-Roll": the outbox, retries, deterministic Message-Id, recipient resolution and per-viewer dismissal already exist and are reused.

Output: the delivery factory extraction, the writer with its resolution record, publisher, service, processor and renderer extensions, resources, tests.
</objective>
