<objective>
Give members honest feedback about long lifecycle jobs (WSP-04, WSP-05): a progress strip that names the current stage of the newest active job without inventing a percentage (D-08), and a failure panel that states what failed, whether the server may still cost money and the next step, with retry, skip, the shared cost-stop and a link to the activity entry (D-09). Plan 63-11 places both in the shell and wires the retry and skip mutation.

Purpose: today the job view shows a milestone `progressPercent` bar and raw tokens; members cannot tell whether a stuck or failed job is still billing them. These two components carry the phase's most safety-relevant wording (cost-stop vocabulary, "may still be costing money"), so they get a pure, exhaustive model and focused component tests.

Output: the stage model, failed-step label and transition announcement in `jobProgress.ts`, the failure model in `jobFailure.ts`, `JobProgressStrip`, `JobFailurePanel`, `job-status.css` and three test files.
</objective>
