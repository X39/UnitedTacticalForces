## Planning Landmines

- Do not add `Recovered` as a fake terminal execution status if the job is actually retry-pending or failed.
- Do not reintroduce `Collect logs` as a visible workspace action.
- Do not make Logs tab opening enqueue work.
- Do not imply runtime cleanup stops provider cost.
- Do not hide provider teardown behind ordinary runtime actions when cost/cleanup risk exists.
- Do not expose worker/provider/Steam credentials or raw secret-bearing payloads through new labels or diagnostics.
