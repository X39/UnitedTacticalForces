| T-35-05 | Elevation of Privilege | active owner comparison | mitigate | Require active assignment to match plan billing account/assignment before reporting usable review state. |

| T-35-07 | Repudiation | cost labels | mitigate | Include cost source label, freshness label, and source timestamp so dry-run estimates are not presented as reconciled billing. |
| T-35-SC | Tampering | package installs | accept | No package-manager installs are planned or allowed. |
